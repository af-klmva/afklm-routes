Ranks unlock routes step by step: Rank 1 Regional, Rank 2 Short Haul, Rank 3 Medium Haul, Rank 4 Long Haul, Rank 5 Ultra Long Haul, Rank 6 adds Codeshares, Rank 7 adds Cargo and Vintage. Each rank includes all routes of the ranks before it. Route type follows leg flight time for KLM, Air France and Transavia: Regional up to 1 hr, Short Haul 1–3:30, Medium Haul 3:30–7, Long Haul 7–11, Ultra Long Haul over 11 hrs; Codeshares, Cargo and Vintage are route types of their own. KLM, Air France and Transavia legs without a flight time in Airtable need Rank 5. Built for the AFKLM virtual airline community; not affiliated with Air France-KLM.
